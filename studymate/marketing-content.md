Copy and structure for the landing page. Organized by section so it maps directly onto components (Hero, Features, HowItWorks, Pricing, FAQ, CTA).

## 1. Hero Section

**Headline:** Turn Any YouTube Video Into a Quiz

**Subheadline:** Paste a link, get a quiz. Test what you actually learned — instantly, from any video.

**Primary CTA:** Try It Free
**Secondary CTA:** See How It Works

**Supporting line (below CTA):** No credit card required to try your first quiz.

## 2. Problem / Positioning

**Section heading:** You watched it. Did you learn it?

**Body:** Millions of people learn from YouTube every day — tutorials, lectures, deep dives. But watching isn't the same as knowing. There's no test, no feedback, no way to check if it actually stuck. We built a way to close that gap: drop in a video, get a real quiz, and find out what you retained.

## 3. How It Works

**Section heading:** From video to quiz in three steps

1. **Paste a YouTube link** — Any video with captions works.
2. **We generate your quiz** — AI reads the transcript and builds multiple-choice questions from the actual content.
3. **Take it and see your score** — Get instant feedback with explanations for every answer.

## 4. Features

**Section heading:** Built for how people actually learn

- **Instant quiz generation** — No manual writing. Questions are pulled straight from the video's content.
- **Explanations, not just answers** — Every question comes with a short explanation so you understand the "why."
- **Works with any topic** — Tutorials, lectures, documentaries, courses — if it has captions, it works.
- **Track your progress** — Save your quiz history and watch your scores improve over time.
- **Essay mode (coming soon)** — Go beyond multiple choice with open-ended questions and AI-graded responses.

## 5. Use Cases

**Section heading:** Who it's for

- **Students** reinforcing lecture or tutorial content
- **Self-taught developers** testing themselves after coding tutorials
- **Lifelong learners** who want to retain what they watch, not just consume it
- **Teams** turning internal training videos into quick comprehension checks

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Does this work on any YouTube video?**
A: It works on any video that has captions (auto-generated or manual). If a video has no captions available, we can't generate a quiz from it yet.

**Q: How accurate are the questions?**
A: Questions are generated directly from the video's transcript, so they're grounded in what's actually said — not outside information.

**Q: Can I use this for essay-style questions?**
A: Essay mode is coming soon. Right now, quizzes are multiple-choice to keep things fast and accurate.

**Q: Is there a free trial for Pro?**
A: The Free plan lets you try the product with 3 quizzes a month, no credit card required. Upgrade to Pro whenever you're ready for unlimited use.
